# Procurement policy requirements

Bidsmith supports supplier preparation and compliance readiness under applicable UK procurement requirements, including Procurement Act 2023 workflows. This is product intent, not a legal certification.

Agent must verify consequential legal/policy statements against current official legislation/GOV.UK and buyer documents. Different jurisdictions, procurement dates, authority scopes and legacy regimes matter; do not assume every UK tender uses one regime.

PPN 017 and PPN 02/24 concern transparency of AI use, not social-value certification. Verify their current scope, effective dates and relationship before implementing rules. Optional questions/policy guidance must not become invented universal supplier duties. Read the actual tender questions.

Maintain source URL/version/review date/effective date/scope/applicability rationale. Separate legislation, policy, buyer instructions and internal checks. Unknown applicability requires human review.

Disclosures reflect actual AI tools/tasks, review and training-data position; yes/no/unknown preserved. No automatic assertions of no-training, review completion or regulatory compliance.
