# Commercial launch plan

A deployed application is not proof of market demand. Deliver an owner-approved controlled pilot or paid release plus measurement, not guaranteed sales.

Target UK suppliers/SMEs and bid consultants. Message: structured, evidence-backed and reviewable tender preparation. No guaranteed wins, automatic legal compliance or invented performance claims.

Owner decisions: legal entity, contact/support, provider/data terms, prices/currency/tax presentation, limits, trial/pilot/payment/refund/cancellation, retention and launch date. Prepare configurable recommendations, never invent approved prices.

Deliver actual landing/features/security/pricing/contact, functional signup/onboarding, labelled synthetic demo tender/company/evidence, usage entitlements, support flow and reviewed privacy/terms/AI notices. If payments enabled, implement verified/idempotent webhooks, scoped customer mapping and cancellation/entitlements. Manual pilot billing requires explicit owner approval, not silent downgrade to a waitlist.

Sequence: internal synthetic demo → owner acceptance → authorised controlled pilot → measured fixes → approved public/paid release. Draft outreach/demo scripts; sending or publishing needs approval.

Measure activation, time to first confirmed requirement/export, user correction effort, job/provider failures, support demand, cost per active organisation and pilot feedback. Establish observed baselines; no fake benchmarks. Analytics contains no confidential document text and must meet reviewed privacy requirements.

Commercial acceptance: new customer understands true offer, joins, completes verified workflow, obtains support and understands data/payment/cancellation terms. Record launch mode and known limitations. Owner signs off.
