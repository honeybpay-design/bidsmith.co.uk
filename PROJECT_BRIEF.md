 # Product baseline

Project/display/workspace name: bidsmith.co.uk. Owner: Donny. New full-stack SaaS implementation.

## Customer and value
UK suppliers, SMEs, bid teams and consultants preparing public-sector tender responses. Reduce fragmented document handling, unsupported claims, repetitive drafting and missed requirements. Position as evidence-backed preparation and compliance readiness, not legal certification or a win predictor.

## Journey
Google sign-in → atomic organisation onboarding → tender workspace → real PDF/DOCX upload → document processing → source-linked requirement extraction → user confirmation → approved knowledge and linked evidence → grounded AI draft → persistent edits and versioned human approval → readiness run → actual DOCX/PDF export → user submission outside the platform.

## Confirmed stack
Next.js App Router/React/TypeScript/Tailwind. Firebase App Hosting. Supabase PostgreSQL/Auth/private Storage/RLS. Google OAuth. GitHub. Google Cloud services where justified. Configurable AI provider/model. Existing resources must be inspected rather than assumed; display names do not prove account state.

## Scope
Include roles, requirements, content library, evidence, drafts/review, disclosure, readiness, exports, usage controls, support, monitored commercial pilot/paid launch. Multi-agent workflows optional, never a blocker to delivery.

## Not initial scope
EU-wide or private-sector legal coverage; buyer award decisions; automated procurement-portal filing; guaranteed wins/compliance; general-purpose agent-builder product; customer-document model training.

## UI direction
Professional English UI; responsive and keyboard usable; requirements navigation, editor, evidence/source panel, visible save/version/review state. Empty/loading/failure/retry states must be real. Accessibility goal WCAG 2.2 AA, not an untested certification claim.

## Owner decisions at relevant gates
Cloud IDs/region/billing, chosen AI/provider terms, legal entity/support contact, retention/recovery objectives, prices/payment or explicitly approved manual pilot, legal review and launch date. Propose configurable defaults but do not invent approved facts. Do not block local foundations on late-stage commercial decisions.
